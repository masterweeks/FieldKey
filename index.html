Property lines

Line color

Auto picks white on Dark, yellow on Aerial, cyan on Hybrid (its roads are yellow) and magenta on Streets and Topo. You can also change it on the map while property lines are on.

Property lines work without this: they use Regrid's free boundary tiles (lines only, from about zoom 12). Paste a parcels proxy endpoint here only if you have a paid Regrid key and want owner, parcel number and acreage when you tap a lot. Takes effect next time you open the map.
